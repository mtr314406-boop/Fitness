# Privacy Policy — Fitness Coach

**Effective: July 2026**

Fitness Coach is a personal, single-user application. It is used only by
its owner, on their own data.

## What it collects
With your explicit OAuth consent, the app reads from your WHOOP account:
recovery scores, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep
duration, and strain. It also reads workout history from your Hevy
account via API key.

## How it's used
Data is stored in a private database controlled by the owner and is used
solely to generate personal training recommendations. Recovery and
training summaries are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce those
recommendations, subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.

## What it does NOT do
- No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.
- No advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
- No data from anyone other than the app's owner is collected.

## Data retention and deletion
Data is retained in the owner's private database and can be deleted at
any time by the owner. Revoking the app's access in WHOOP settings stops
all future collection immediately.
